Thinking a lot about goals and ambitions lately. There's this pressure we put on ourselves to always be climbing, always reaching for the next thing. But when does it stop? When are you allowed to be satisfied?

Had a conversation today that made me reconsider a few things. My friend asked if I was happy, not just busy. It hit different. Busy doesn't equal happy, and I think I've been confusing the two for a while now.

Work is going well, but it's not fulfilling in the way I want. There's something missing. Maybe it's the purpose behind it. Maybe I need to remember why I started this in the first place.

Started a new habit this week – writing for twenty minutes every morning. It's not much, but it's something. The ideas flow better when you don't overthink. That's always the trap, isn't it? We overthink everything.

Exercise is becoming easier. The body adapts quickly, I'm realizing. What felt impossible three weeks ago is routine now. There's something profound in that. We're more capable than we think.

The evenings are getting longer. There's more light, which changes the whole mood. I spend more time outside, less time scrolling. Small things matter more than we admit.
