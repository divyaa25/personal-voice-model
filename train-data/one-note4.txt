Being 20 is not easy, you go through existential crisis, you question everything you do, everyday you wake up and argue with yourself about whether to get out of bed and work or rot in bed all day. But whatever that 10 year old you had to go through wasn't easy either.
When you were in class 5, when you were so naïve you believed everything they told you. To a 10 year old, grown adults look like superhumans. They seem like they know it all. Whatever they do somehow looks correct even if it is morally incorrect. You look up to them and respect them. They told you that you needed to be fair and have silky straight hair to be termed as beautiful. And you, like everyone wanted to be that. Not being able to meet those standards downgraded your self-esteem and you thought you don't deserve love. You looked at others and thought to yourself, oh so gorgeous, hating yourself more and more in the process.
 
They told you that you are not good enough and you believed it. Your peers and relatives, with their manipulative ways, were able to convince you that your actions were not girly enough. And again, as any 10 year old would, you believed it. Constructive criticism is one thing and brainwashing is the other.
 
Well, look at you now. You have come a long way.
We change drastically as we grow up. The physical growth obviously being of no surprise, the mental and emotional maturity is sure a big deal. It never fails to surprise me whenever I look back. How did we go from being a naïve little kid, who believed everything they said, to a grown, mature individual who doesn't care about what people have to say about them. A person, to whom things like self-care, self-love and productivity matter more than anything in life. Beautiful.
