Growth and growing up are two different things. Growth is not when you're able to fit in your father's shoes or in your mother's kurtis but it's when you realise that it’s the little things in life that matter the most, it's the most smallest things that make the significant differences. Growth is when you find serenity in the mundane activities, when the sound of dripping rain soothes you, when you realise that love is in the petrichor, that when the drops of rain meet the soil, like two lovers that have been apart for so long and now their union has produced a smell that heals the nature.
